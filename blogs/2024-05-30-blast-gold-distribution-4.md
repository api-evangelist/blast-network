10 million Gold will be distributed to Dapps in the fourth distribution. This is the penultimate Dapp gold distribution ahead of the Blast airdrop June 26. Methodology Dapps live on Mainnet were weighted based on the following formula: Category Weight x Blast-native Weight x Dapp Weight x Incentive Weight.
